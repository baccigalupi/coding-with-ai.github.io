## About Coding-with.ai!

[Coding-with.ai](http://www.coding-with.ai) is about coding with AI. Generative AI is a powerful tool that is addictive in its abilities. Yet the commercial aspects of the industry gaslight us about its capabilities, selling executives impossible dreams of productivity. That leaves most developers wondering what they should be doing differently, how they are failing.

This blog is aspirational. I think that agents and models should be better. I have ideas, and those ideas could keep us from accelerating global destruction via data farms too. The blog is
practical; I've been doing experiments with a variety of agents and models to see what's behind the curtain. That way we can know how to use them today and sketch out a better future.
